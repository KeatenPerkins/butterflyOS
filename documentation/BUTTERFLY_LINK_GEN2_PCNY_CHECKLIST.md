# Gen II PCNY remaining-gift test checklist

The final documented-catalog expansion adds 79 actual eggs and 16 shiny adult
gifts. The user confirmed all 95 additions and the split egg/adult menu working
in Crystal on .17. Together with earlier batches, all 104 PCNY eggs, 16 shiny
adult gifts and 15 Japanese Mystery Eggs passed user gameplay testing.

Reopen Butterfly Link → Local transfer → Gen 2 → PCNY Gifts. Choose **Special-Move Eggs** (104) or **Shiny Adult Gifts** (16). New eggs
append after the first 25 within the egg list. Close the game before editing,
choose a free PC slot, review and Commit. Withdraw an egg, hatch it, check its
special move, save and reload. For shiny adults, check level, moves and shiny
appearance, then save and reload. Eggs have random DVs and natural shiny odds;
the specifically named shiny gifts are guaranteed shiny. Generated gifts use
your trainer identity, not the historical machine's OT/ID.

## Catalog coverage and limits

Of 136 catalog entries, 120 have PCNY menu choices: 104 eggs and 16 shiny adults.
Fourteen identical recipes already appear in Mystery Eggs. Celebi is covered by
the Gold/Silver gift and Crystal's native GS Ball quest. The remaining non-shiny
Suicune entry lacks documented level/moves and is unavailable. This completes
the implementable entries in this catalog, not every possible Gen II gift or
native in-game gift replay. Silver Celebi and broader Gold/Silver gameplay still
need qualification. Historical egg shiny rates, campaign dates and donor records
are not recreated.

Recipe inventory: [PCNY distribution catalog](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/List_of_PCNY_event_Pok%C3%A9mon_distributions_in_Generation_II).
Technical fields are checked against primary [pret/pokecrystal game data](https://github.com/pret/pokecrystal).
No ROM, extracted sprite, donor save or premade Pokémon record is bundled.
