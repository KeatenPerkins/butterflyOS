# Commit attribution

The maintainer does not want AI tools listed as Git co-authors. Do not add
`Co-Authored-By` trailers for AI assistants.
Preserve human author credits and upstream attribution. This does not remove
requirements to disclose AI assistance in reviews or forms that request it.

Published history was cleaned on October 22, 2026. Do not merge or push old
history that reintroduces the removed trailers; see
`docs/history/COMMIT_ID_MAP_20261022.json` when migrating old work.
